About Us

Learn more about our journey, mission, and the people behind Shisa City Community Kitchen

Our History

Shisa City Community Kitchen was established in 2015 by a group of local residents concerned about food insecurity in the city's eastern suburbs. What began as a weekly soup kitchen operating from a church hall has grown into a comprehensive community food service.

Over the past decade, the organisation has expanded to operate a permanent facility with a commercial kitchen, a community dining space, and a small food garden. The organisation now serves over 500 meals weekly and provides cooking classes, food literacy programs, and emergency food relief to vulnerable community members.

Today, Shisa City Community Kitchen is a beloved local institution, known for its commitment to nourishing the community, using locally sourced ingredients, and fostering a welcoming atmosphere for all.

Our Mission & Vision

🎯 Our Mission

To nourish our community by providing access to healthy, affordable food while building connections and food skills among residents.

🌟 Our Vision

A community where everyone has access to nutritious food and the knowledge to prepare it, fostering dignity, connection, and wellbeing.

Opened permanent facility with commercial kitchen and community dining space.
