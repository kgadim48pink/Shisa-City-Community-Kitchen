Get Involved

Your support makes a difference. Join us in nourishing our community.

There are many ways you can support Shisa City Community Kitchen. Whether you have time to give, funds to donate, or resources to share, your contribution helps us feed more families and build a stronger community.

🤝 Volunteer With Us

Volunteers are the heart of our organisation. We welcome individuals and groups who want to make a difference in our community.

Volunteer Opportunities

  • Kitchen Assistant: Help prepare and serve meals
  • Garden Helper: Maintain our community garden
  • Event Support: Assist with community events and workshops
  • Administrative Support: Help with office tasks and data entry
  • Delivery Driver: Deliver meals to homebound community members

Benefits of Volunteering

  • ❤️ Make a meaningful difference in your community
  • 🤝 Meet new people and build connections
  • 🎓 Learn new skills and gain experience
  • 🍽️ Enjoy a free meal during your shift
  • 📜 Receive a volunteer certificate

💰 Make a Donation

Your financial support helps us provide nutritious meals, run cooking classes, and maintain our community garden. Every contribution makes a difference.

Donation Options

  • One-Time Donation: Make a single contribution
  • Monthly Donation: Become a regular supporter
  • In-Kind Donation: Donate food, supplies, or equipment

Your Impact

  • R50 = Provides a meal for a family of 4
  • R100 = Supplies ingredients for a cooking class
  • R250 = Provides garden supplies for a month
  • R500 = Supports a week of meal services
