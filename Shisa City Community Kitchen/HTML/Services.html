Our Programs

Discover the range of services we offer to nourish and empower our community

At Shisa City Community Kitchen, we offer a variety of programs designed to address food insecurity, build essential skills, and foster community connections. All our programs are free and open to everyone in our community.

Find a Program

🍲

Meal Program

Daily Service

Our flagship program provides hot, nutritious meals to community members in need. Meals are prepared fresh daily using locally sourced ingredients and are served in our welcoming community dining space.

We currently serve over 500 meals weekly, ensuring that no one in our community goes to bed hungry.

  • 📅 Monday - Friday
  • 🕐 12:00 PM - 2:00 PM
  • 👥 Open to all
  • 🍽️ 500+ meals weekly
👩‍🍳

Cooking Classes

Weekly Classes

Learn essential cooking skills with our free, hands-on cooking classes. Our experienced chefs guide participants through preparing healthy, affordable meals using simple ingredients and techniques.

Classes are designed for all skill levels, from complete beginners to those looking to expand their culinary knowledge.

  • 📅 Every Thursday
  • 🕐 6:00 PM - 8:00 PM
  • 👥 15 participants per class
  • 🎓 All skill levels welcome
📚

Food Literacy Program

Monthly Workshops

Our Food Literacy Program empowers community members with knowledge about nutrition, meal planning, and smart grocery shopping. Participants learn how to make healthy food choices on a budget and understand the importance of a balanced diet.

Topics covered include reading food labels, understanding nutritional information, and planning nutritious meals for families.

  • 📅 First Saturday of each month
  • 🕐 10:00 AM - 12:00 PM
  • 👥 20 participants per session
  • 📖 Educational materials provided
🌱

Community Garden

Growing Together

Our community garden produces fresh, organic vegetables and herbs that supplement our meal program. Community members are invited to participate in planting, maintaining, and harvesting the garden.

The garden serves as an educational space where participants learn about sustainable food production, composting, and the benefits of growing your own food.

  • 📅 Open daily
  • 🕐 8:00 AM - 6:00 PM
  • 👥 Volunteers welcome
  • 🌿 Organic produce
